HOW SEARCH WORKS

Evidence first.
Limits included.

SignalScout combines a public geocoder and community map data. It does not independently verify every business record.

  1. Choose a place. The browser sends the location text to Open-Meteo Geocoding API and gets a place coordinate.
  2. Search nearby map features. SignalScout requests the selected business categories within your chosen radius from the OpenStreetMap Overpass API.
  3. Prioritize websites. Results require a business name and a publicly mapped website tag. Listings with phone, email, or opening hours appear first when available.
  4. Inspect the source. Each result links to its OpenStreetMap object. Details are community-contributed and may be stale or incorrect.
  5. Export locally. JSON and CSV files are downloaded by your browser; the hosted site does not store those exports.

Coverage is uneven

OpenStreetMap is not a complete business registry. Results are shaped by local contributors, tagging practices, query radius, and provider limits. A missing result or website tag is not proof the business or website does not exist.

No live SEO score in browser

The browser scanner does not silently fetch or audit each business website. PageSpeed Insights remains an optional Python CLI feature; scores are snapshots and must not be presented as rankings or guarantees.

Responsible access

Requests go directly from your browser to third-party services. Use reasonable search volumes, follow provider usage policies, respect OSM attribution and ODbL, and do not use results for sensitive personal-data collection or automated unsolicited outreach. See the data-source directory and Terms.
